      ### what is information theory?

      Every message — a text, a song, a photo, your DNA — can be measured in bits. Information theory asks: how much information is really in there, and how efficiently can we send it, store it, or protect it from noise?

      The key idea is surprise. If you already know what comes next, it carries no information. A string like aaaaaaaa is perfectly predictable: zero surprise. A coin flip carries exactly one bit.
